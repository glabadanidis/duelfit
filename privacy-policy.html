Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

DuelFit ("we", "our", or "us") operates the DuelFit mobile application (the "App"). This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, who can see it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account and use DuelFit we collect:

  • Account information — your full name, username, and email address.
  • Profile photo — optional. If you add one, it is stored and shown to other DuelFit users.
  • Challenge activity — the matches you pick, your predictions, challenge results, the forfeits you agree to, and whether each forfeit was delivered or missed.
  • Forfeit proof — photos or videos you choose to upload, or links you add (for example a Strava activity), to show that you did a forfeit.
  • Friends — friend requests you send and accept.
  • Invite code — optional. If you sign up with someone's invite code, we record who invited you.
  • Push notification token — a device token used to send you notifications about your challenges and friend requests.

We do not collect payment information, location data, your contacts, or any health or fitness data from your device. The App asks for access to your photo library or camera only when you choose to add a profile photo or a proof, and only uses the items you pick.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To run challenges: settle results from public match data, and track forfeits and proofs.
  • To send push notifications about your challenge and friend activity.
  • To understand how people find DuelFit, using invite codes.
  • To keep the App secure, and to improve it and fix issues.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

3. What Other Users Can See

  • Any signed-in DuelFit user can see your username, full name, profile photo, and your reliability (how many of your forfeits you delivered or missed), and can find you by searching your username.
  • The other player in a challenge can see that challenge, its forfeit, and any proof you upload for it.
  • Who invited whom is not shown to anyone in the App.

4. Data Storage

Your data, including profile photos and proofs, is stored using Supabase, a cloud database provider, on servers located in the European Union. Supabase's privacy policy is available at supabase.com/privacy.

5. Service Providers

We do not share, sell, or rent your personal information to any third party, except these providers that process data on our behalf, and only to run the App:

  • Supabase — database, file storage, and authentication.
  • Expo, together with Apple Push Notification service and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — deliver push notifications. They receive your device token and the notification text.
  • TheSportsDB — provides match fixtures and results. We send it no personal information.
  • Legal requirements — we may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

6. Your Rights

You may request at any time:

  • Access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction of inaccurate data.
  • Deletion of your account and all associated data.

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time in the App under Settings > Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and profile. You can also email us to request deletion of everything associated with your account, including uploaded photos and proofs, and we will complete it within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

DuelFit is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
