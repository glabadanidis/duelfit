## Description (4000 chars max)

Think you know football better than your friends? Prove it.

DuelFit turns sports predictions into personal challenges between friends. Pick an upcoming match, choose your side, dare someone to disagree — and make them pay when you're right.

No money. No betting. Just real physical forfeits.

**HOW IT WORKS**

1. Browse upcoming matches from top leagues — Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, NBA, and more.
2. Pick your prediction: home win, away win, or draw.
3. Set a forfeit — 5km run, 50 push-ups, cold shower, or anything you agree on.
4. Challenge a friend. They pick the opposite side.
5. When the match ends, the loser does the forfeit. No excuses.

**FEATURES**

⚔️ Challenge any friend on any upcoming match
🏆 Leaderboard — see who rules your circle
📊 Track your wins, losses, and win rate
🔔 Instant notifications when challenges are sent or accepted
📅 Live match schedule across multiple leagues
👤 Personal profile with full challenge history

**THE SPIRIT OF DUELFIT**

DuelFit isn't about gambling — it's about accountability, banter, and making sport personal. The stakes are physical. The satisfaction is real. And the shame of doing 100 burpees in front of your mates? Priceless.

Challenge accepted?
